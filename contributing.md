# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping keep this list useful.

## What belongs here

- **Upwork-specific tools and resources.** General freelancing tools, generic job boards, and all-purpose scrapers belong on other lists.
- Anything genuinely useful to someone working with Upwork: freelancers, agencies, recruiters, and developers building on Upwork data.
- Open source and commercial entries are both welcome. Commercial entries must state their pricing model in the description.

## What does not belong here

- Tools that do not work, are abandoned, or whose links are dead.
- Services that require sharing your Upwork password with a third party.
- Entries added purely for promotion with no use to a reader.

## Format

Add your entry to the **end of the relevant section**, in this format:

```markdown
- [Name](url) - One-line description ending with a period.
```

- Keep descriptions to a single line, factual, and free of marketing language.
- Mention pricing when a tool is paid ("$4 per 1,000 rows", "subscription", "free tier").
- Do not add tracking or referral parameters to links.

## Pull requests

- One entry per pull request where possible.
- Explain briefly what the tool does and why it is useful, if it is not obvious.
- Check that your link resolves before submitting.
- Search the list first so you do not duplicate an existing entry.

## Removals

If you own a tool listed here and would like it removed, open an issue. Removal
requests from tool owners are honoured without argument or delay.

If you find a dead link, an inaccurate description, or a tool that has been
abandoned, please open an issue or a pull request. Keeping the list accurate
matters more than keeping it long.
